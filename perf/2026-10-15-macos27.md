# The GPU battery on macOS 27, with the Metal transport's desktop

**Subject:** the venus and native-context (Metal) transports measured again after the host moved to
macOS 27 and the transport gained the vendor-driver desktop, its sync fixes and the host vsync grid.
A third arm runs the enhanced image on the transport, the way the dogfood runs it.
- **Host:** macOS 27.0.1, M1 Max; limina-kk `2e1d2485bca`; virglrs `2efa024`; libkrun `28d988ca`;
  limina `e696bb19`.
- **Guests, 4 vCPUs, 4 GiB, 1280x800 @ 1.0, 60 Hz:**
  - `Fedora-Workstation-44.metal.test.raw`: stock kernel `6.19.10-300.fc44` at 4 KiB pages, Fedora
    Mesa, and the transport's vendor drivers with module `a5ab977e7ab2`. Used by the venus and metal
    arms.
  - `Fedora-Workstation-44.enhanced.test.raw`: the 16 KiB `7.1.13` kernel and limina Mesa, with the
    vendor drivers. Used by the enhanced arm.

Drivers, rows and evidence are in `perf/macos27-2026-10-15/`; nothing went to `perf/ledger.csv`.

## What this pass can and cannot see

- **Arms.**
  - **venus:** every GPU workload on the venus ICD, GL through zink on venus, and the desktop on
    vrend.
  - **metal:** the worker started with `LIMINA_GPU_METAL=1`. The vendor drivers take gnome-shell and
    every client with no environment set, so the desktop itself runs on the transport, unlike
    2026-10-13.
  - **enhanced:** like metal, on the enhanced image, with Firefox on hardware WebRender. The venus
    and metal arms run the aquarium under software WebRender, as on 2026-10-13.
- **Order.** The main sweep ran v0 m0 v1 m1 v2, then e0 m2 e1, so each arm is bracketed by the one
  it is compared with.
- **Host health.**
  - The `gl-replay-llvmpipe` control read 728-750 throughout. No point aborted or wedged, and no
    worker log has a poisoned-context line.
  - The control reads ~8% above 2026-10-13's 677-691. Two other VMs were up that day, and none on
    this one, so the CPU-side baseline moved by about that much. That is not a GPU effect.
- **Not a clean macOS 26 → 27 A/B.** Host and guest builds moved too: the transport's sync fixes,
  the vendor-driver desktop and zink off its implicit-sync wait all landed after 2026-10-13. The
  venus arm, which barely changed, is the closest thing to an OS-only comparison.

## Results

Eight points, one boot each, measured 2026-10-15:

| workload | venus (v0 v1 v2) | metal (m0 m1 m2) | enhanced (e0 e1) | metal vs venus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gl-replay-llvmpipe (control) | 735 734 735 | 731 728 734 | 732 750 | — |
| gl-replay | 50.3 51.6 51.7 | 51.4 51.6 51.9 | 51.5 51.7 | same |
| vk-replay headless | 1864 1856 1852 | *676* 2168 2135 | 2151 2067 | **+16%** |
| glmark2-wayland | 2333 2225 2069 | 3271 3245 3283 | 3340 3322 | **+47%** |
| vkmark (median of 9 runs; 6 for enhanced) | 2082 | 4159 | 3940 | **+100%** |
| aquarium 25k | 26 26, 26 26, 28 27 | 36 39, 38 42, 36 38 | 45 50, 48 43 | **+46%** |
| aquarium 30k | 23 23, 23 22, 23 22 | 35 34, 35 35, 35 34 | 40 42, 38 40 | **+52%** |

- **The transport now wins every GPU workload but gl-replay.**
  - Since 2026-10-13, headless vk-replay went from 1466 to 2151 (+47%), glmark2-wayland from 1085
    to 3271 (3x), and vkmark from 2210 to 4159 (+88%). The vk-replay gain is what the guest KK's
    sync-file fix (`dce6d5542a8`) predicted.
  - glmark2-wayland is the +45% the all-zink check on 2026-10-13 predicted, now on the stock image
    with the desktop on the transport.
  - vkmark nearly doubling is new. vkmark's frame was ~0.45 ms on both arms, and a CPU wait per
    present would cost about that.
- **The aquarium did not move on the transport** (38/35 against 38.5/35) and stays half again ahead
  of venus. It is paced by per-command cost, which was already the transport's strength.
- **Venus is flat** against 2026-10-13: vk-replay 1856 against 1845, glmark2 2225 against 2200,
  aquarium 26/23 against 25/22. vkmark is 4% lower (2082 against 2176). So macOS 27 neither helped
  nor hurt the venus path measurably.
- **The enhanced image adds hardware WebRender.** The aquarium reads +22% at 25k and +14% at 30k
  over the metal arm. The other workloads are level with metal; vkmark is 5% lower. That arm differs
  in kernel, page size, guest Mesa and the WebRender mode at once, so the gain is not attributed to
  any one of them.
- **gl-replay does not move**, on any arm. It is paced by the Xwayland present path.

## Found on the way

- **m0's headless vk-replay read 676, a third of m1 and m2.** It was the first metal point after the
  image's module changed. The first attempt at this pass showed the same on its first venus point
  (642 against 1850 later), after that arm's image had changed. A cold guest pipeline cache would
  explain both, but that is not verified. The host shader cache directories of the metal arm are
  empty, so a host cache does not. The metal column's comparison uses m1 and m2.
- **The metal arm wedged at boot on macOS 27, four boots in four.**
  - The 6.19 virtio-gpu that the module vendors raised the display hotplug from the control queue's
    dequeue worker. The console's re-commit then waited on its primary-plane flush fence, which only
    that worker signals.
  - Fixed by backporting upstream `d1b894c5bbb3` into the module (`a5ab977e`). Guarded by
    `metal_window_boot`, the only metal test with a window, which is what sends the display
    re-announce that triggers the hotplug.
  - The sysrq dump of the wedged guest is in `evidence/wedge-before-hotplug-fix/`.
  - The enhanced images carry an older module without the backport. Their 7.1 kernel's virtio-gpu
    already has the fix, so they do not wedge.
- **The enhanced arm's first-run Firefox shows a notification bar** above the aquarium canvas on the
  first run of each point. The fps counter stayed legible and the canvas size is unchanged.
