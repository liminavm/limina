# The GPU and compute battery after the 2026-11 fork rebase

**Subject:** a trend reading of the whole stack after the forks were rebased (libkrun, virglrs,
both Mesa branches, the guest kernel). There is no pre-rebase arm, so this pass can show a large
regression against the October passes but cannot attribute one.
- **Host:** macOS 27.0.1, M1 Max; limina `f84263d2` (compute pass 2: `dd2c302e`), libkrun
  `9415e900`, virglrs `fd626a9`, host KosmicKrisp `2b27e76a1d7`. No other VM was up.
- **Guest:** `Fedora-Workstation-44.enhanced.test.raw` at payload r47 (the 16 KiB `7.3.5` kernel,
  Fedora Mesa, vendor drivers `c0a873da495` at wire 5); 4 vCPUs, 4 GiB, 1280x800 @ 1.0, 60 Hz.
  Compute ran on `Fedora-Workstation-44.metal.test.raw` as `scripts/perf-compute.sh` sets it up.

Scripts and evidence are in `perf/battery-2026-11-22/`; the rows are in `perf/ledger.csv`.

## What this pass can and cannot see

- **Tiers.** `metal`: the worker with `LIMINA_GPU_METAL=1`, every GPU workload on the vendor
  drivers' KosmicKrisp and zink on it. `vrend`: the desktop on vrend over host zink, the GL rows
  through zink on the distro's venus, Vulkan on venus. Each point's `arm.txt` proves its tier
  before measuring (vendor drivers rev, the Vulkan device, the GL renderer, and on vrend the host
  libgallium); none aborted, wedged or tripped the poisoned-context watchdog.
- **Order:** m0 v0 m1 v1 m2 v2, then the compute half, then m3 and compute pass 2. A shakedown pair
  before m0 warmed each tier's shader cache and is not counted.
- **Host health:** the `gl-replay-llvmpipe` control read 725-742 at every point.
- **The baseline differs in more than the rebase.** The October enhanced arm ran the `7.1.13`
  kernel; this image runs `7.3.5`. A shift here is the stack's, not the rebase's.

## Compute

Pass 1 (after m0-v2) against `perf/2026-10-18-compute.md`, guest / native:

- **Level:** MLX matmul f16 (14.71 / 14.69 ms), mlx-lm 0.5B and 7B (7B generation 0.92 of native,
  as in October), command buffers/s (0.98), copy bandwidth (0.96), dispatches/s (0.42), the
  two-queue overlap.
- **Commit round trip p90 is higher:** 388 µs in the guest against 319-340, native 258 against
  238-243; the medians are level (288 / 228). Pass 2 reads 397 / 269.
- **MLX matmul f32 read 2.57 ms in the guest (0.86 of native)** in pass 1 and 2.32 ms in pass 2,
  against 2.28-2.29 in October. A one-off of the kind October's f16 showed.
- **llama.cpp's guest rows are in pass 2 only.** Pass 2's guest reads 3070 / 172 tok/s (0.5B
  pp512 / tg128) and 225 / 24.6 (7B), level with October's 3027 / 167 and 228 / 25.2.
- **Pass 2's native control is not usable.** Native read up to 25% below its own pass-1 run (llama
  0.5B pp512 2302 against 3061, f16 matmul 18.4 ms against 14.7), so its native and of-native rows
  carry a note saying so; its guest rows stand.

## Found on the way

- **The distro's venus crashes the vk-replay trace.** Fedora's Mesa 26.2.7 `libvulkan_virtio.so`
  segfaults in `virtgpu_submit` (from `vn_QueueSubmit`, in gfxreconstruct's swapchain image
  transition after `vkAcquireNextImageKHR`) on `gfxrecon-replay --wsi headless vkcube.gfxr`. It
  does so on `enhanced.test`, `metal.test` and `stock.test` (stock kernel, stock module), every
  time. limina's venus replays the same trace, and vkmark runs on the distro's venus. It is
  Fedora's Mesa, between 26.1.8 and 26.2.6, not the host or the kernel
  (`spikes/venus-replay-crash/RESULTS.md`). The battery records `vk-replay-limina-venus-headless`
  instead.
- **Rows recorded on the wrong ICD.** Before `dd2c302e`, perf-ledger's metal tier and the compute
  guest picked the newest `limina-*.json`, which is `limina-venus.json`. The metal tier's vk-replay
  rows of m0-m2 are relabelled `vk-replay-limina-venus-headless` in the ledger; pass 1 has no guest
  llama.cpp rows.
