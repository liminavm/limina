# A/B: the 2026-10-02 rebase

**Subject:** the whole rebase.
- **Host:** limina-kk `d39e8bff0f3`; virglrs `880cac7` on the rebased C fork `ad8e1b75`; libkrun `5905b876`.
- **Guest:** mesa `26.2.3-2.limina` and kernel `7.1.13-limina16k`, payload r30.

The guest half is a bracketed A/B on the rebased host. The host half is read against
`perf/kk-alloc-pool-2026-10-01/` (the same instruments on the pre-rebase host, the day before).
Drivers, rows and evidence are in `perf/rebase-2026-10-02/`; nothing went to `perf/ledger.csv`.

## What this pass can and cannot see

- **One guest:** enhanced F44. The two arms differ only in the image:
  - **old** is `Fedora-Workstation-44.enhanced.bak-pre-r28.raw`: mesa `26.1.8-11.limina`, kernel `7.1.8-limina16k.4`.
  - **new** is `Fedora-Workstation-44.enhanced.raw` on r30.
  - Each point's `settle-enh.txt` records `uname -r` and the installed mesa, so the arms are proven, not assumed.
- **Guest components:** the new guest changes mesa and the kernel together, so a guest-side
  difference cannot be split between them.
- **Workloads:** the aquarium is the vrend reading (the guest's virgl driver over zink-on-KK);
  perf-ledger and vkmark are venus.
- **Host health:** the `gl-replay-llvmpipe` control read 727-733 in every point (724-732 the
  day before), so the host was quiet and the two days are comparable.
- **Logs:** no point aborted or wedged, and no worker log has a poisoned, FATAL or device-lost line.

- **The vkmark lift is uniform.** Every scene rises from about 2550 to about 3300, whatever it draws
  (`evidence/*/vkmark-run1.txt`). That is a per-frame overhead bound moving, not one scene changing
  cost, and it fits mesa 26.2's venus submission and sync rework.
- **gl-replay-venus is 2-3% lower on the new guest** (43.9-44.9 against 45.2-45.4). Both new points
  are below all three old ones. It is small and has not been chased.
- **The aquarium is unchanged at 30k.** New reads 38-44, old 37-43.
- **Two 25k readings in point n1 read 3 and 16 fps.** n0's 25k reads 50 and 46, and n1's 30k
  captures from the same runs read 38 and 39. Nothing in that point's worker log separates it.
  Read it as a transient caught by the capture until a rerun says otherwise; it is the one reading
  in this pass that a follow-up should repeat.
