# The Metal transport before and after a host queue per command queue (wire VERSION 5)

**Subject:** wire VERSION 5 gives every guest `MTLCommandQueue` a host queue of its own, and a
fence names the queue's completed value (`docs/design/metal-transport-queues.md`). This pass asks
whether that cost the guest's KosmicKrisp anything on the workloads the battery measures.
- **Host:** macOS 27.0.1, M1 Max; limina-kk `059715d2aa5`; virglrs `2efa024`. One stack per arm:
  - **v4:** limina `e62ea94a`, libkrun `9dc32a05`.
  - **v5:** limina `9a2e5558`, libkrun `ce663d74`.
- **Guest:** `Fedora-Workstation-44.metal.test.raw` (stock kernel `6.19.10-300.fc44`, 4 KiB pages,
  Fedora Mesa, limina's vendor GPU drivers); 4 vCPUs, 4 GiB, 1280x800 @ 1.0, 60 Hz.
  - **v4:** its pre-delivery backup, `/opt/limina/4` (guest KK `41353793c95`).
  - **v5:** as delivered, `/opt/limina/5` (guest KK `340ed84bf1b`) and module `52f5b04e865d`.

Drivers, rows and evidence are in `perf/wire-v5-2026-10-17/`; nothing went to `perf/ledger.csv`.

## What this pass can and cannot see

- **Each arm is a whole stack.** Host and guest drivers move together. A VERSION 4 guest on a
  VERSION 5 host, or the reverse, declines the transport and runs stock. Each point's `arm.txt`
  proves its arm: KosmicKrisp as the Vulkan device, zink on it at the arm's guest KK rev, only that
  wire's `/opt/limina/<wire>` installed, and gnome-shell mapping that wire's driver. A point
  without all four would have aborted; none did.
- **Both arms are the 2026-10-15 metal arm:** the desktop and every client on the vendor drivers,
  with no environment set. The aquarium runs Firefox under software WebRender, as on 2026-10-15.
- **Order:** b0 a0 b1 a1 b2 (b = v4, a = v5), so the baseline is measured at both ends.
- **Host health:** the `gl-replay-llvmpipe` control read 730-740 throughout. No other VM was up,
  no point aborted or wedged, and no worker log has a poisoned-context line.
- **Sensitivity.** These instruments catch a large regression, such as a lost overlap or a wait
  per frame. They cannot see a small fence-path cost: on venus the whole fence path was 1.8% of
  the worker's wall time (`perf/2026-09-10-sow.md`). The direct measure of VERSION 5 is the shim's
  two-queue test (`spikes/metal-cpp-shim/RESULTS.md`): one kernel on each of two queues takes
  1.00x one kernel's time, where the same two on one queue take 2.00x.

## Results

Five points, one boot each, measured 2026-10-17:

| workload | v4 (b0 b1 b2) | v5 (a0 a1) | v5 vs v4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| gl-replay-llvmpipe (control) | 738 740 740 | 735 730 | — |
| gl-replay | 50.9 51.0 51.0 | 51.3 51.3 | same |
| vk-replay headless | *687* 2093 2146 | 2108 2122 | same |
| glmark2-wayland | 3387 3349 3389 | 3370 3403 | same |
| vkmark (3 runs a point) | 4272-4286, 4266-4299, 4266-4281 | 4264-4274, 4233-4551 | same |
| aquarium 25k | 37 39, 38 38, 36 39 | 38 37, 38 38 | same |
| aquarium 30k | 35 35, 34 34, 34 32 | 35 34, 35 35 | same |

- **No workload separates the arms.** Every v5 value sits inside the band v4 spans around it.
- **b0's Vulkan replay (687) is the first boot of an image after its GPU module changed.** The
  same thing happened to the first metal point on 2026-10-15 (676). It is left out of the
  comparison.
- **a1's vkmark stepped up mid-run.** The run started level with every other point (4233), then
  every scene from the middle of run 2 on read about 7% faster (4411, 4551). a0 on the same stack
  did not do it, and b2 after it read 4266-4281. Nothing attributes the step, so it is not
  counted for either arm.
- **Against 2026-10-15's metal arm, this pass is level.** That arm read vk-replay 2135-2168,
  glmark2 3245-3283, vkmark 4159 and aquarium 36-42/34-35 under VERSION 4 two days earlier.
  vkmark reads ~3% higher here on both arms alike.
