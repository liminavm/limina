# GPU compute over the Metal transport, against native: the first baseline

**Subject:** the first rows of `scripts/perf-compute.sh` (perf/README.md §Compute). It measures
three tiers in a guest on the Metal transport and then the same work natively, one after the
other on the same GPU.
- **Host:** macOS 27.0.1, M1 Max 32 GB. limina `40522aed`, libkrun `ce663d74`, virglrs `2efa024`,
  host KosmicKrisp `059715d2aa5`. No other VM was up and nothing else was running.
- **Guest:** a fresh clone of `Fedora-Workstation-44.metal.test.raw` per pass, at wire 5. It has
  the stock `6.19.10` kernel, 4 KiB pages, 8 vCPUs and 16 GiB, with the compositor stopped.
- **Pins:** MLX `4cb085b`, mlx-lm 0.32.2, llama.cpp `b42b7e6d`. The models are Qwen2.5-Instruct
  0.5B and 7B, as MLX 4-bit and GGUF Q4_0.

Measured 2026-10-18, two passes. The rows are in `perf/ledger.csv` and the logs in
`perf/compute-2026-10-18/`.

## Results

`of-native` is guest speed over native speed, so 1.00 is native.

| workload | guest | native | of-native (pass 1, 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| llama-bench 0.5B pp512 / tg128 | 3027 / 167 tok/s | 3029 / 168 | 1.00, 1.00 / 1.00, 0.99 |
| llama-bench 7B pp512 / tg128 | 228 / 25.2 | 229 / 24.3 | 1.00, 0.99 / 1.03, 1.05 |
| MLX matmul f32 2048³ | 2.28 ms | 2.22 | 0.97, 0.97 |
| MLX matmul f16 4096³ | 14.7, *17.6* ms | 14.7 | 1.00, *0.84* |
| mlx-lm 0.5B prompt / generation | 8.7k / 302 tok/s | 8.1k / 299 | 1.08, 1.07 / 1.03, 0.99 |
| mlx-lm 7B prompt / generation | 339 / 52 | 369 / 57 | 0.92, 0.92 / 0.92, 0.90 |
| metal commit round trip, median / p90 | 274-292 / 319-340 µs | 227-229 / 238-243 | 0.83, 0.78 / 0.75, 0.71 |
| metal command buffers / s | 42k | 43k | 0.98, 0.98 |
| metal dispatches / s (10 000 in one buffer) | 1.95M | 4.82M | 0.40, 0.41 |
| metal copy bandwidth (256 MiB) | 309 GB/s | 325 | 0.95, 0.95 |
| metal two queues against one | 1.00x | 2.00x | — |

- **llama.cpp on the guest's KosmicKrisp runs at native speed.** Prompt processing is within 1%
  and token generation within 5%. The guest is faster at 7B generation in both passes. The
  host's llama.cpp compiles its shaders with a newer glslc (2026.4 against Fedora's 2026.1), and
  the two KosmicKrisp builds are on different revisions, so a few percent either way is not the
  transport.
- **MLX's GPU-bound work is at native speed**: both matmuls, and the 0.5B model end to end.
  The 0.5B prompt rows read 7% above native in both passes; nothing here says why.
- **MLX's 7B is 8-10% slower in the guest**, prompt and generation alike, in both passes. That
  is the one end-to-end gap the battery shows. It is a lead, not a diagnosis.
- **Pass 2's f16 matmul (0.84) is a one-off.** Pass 1 and the native runs read 14.7 ms, and
  nothing else in pass 2 moved.
- **Packing dispatches is where the transport costs most:** 0.40 of native. A single dispatch
  costs 47-63 µs more round trip at the median and 81-97 µs at p90. Command-buffer throughput
  and bandwidth are within 5%.
- **The two-queue row is not a cost.** The guest runs two queues side by side, while native
  classic Metal, which is what the bench and MLX on macOS use, takes turns. The host's MTL4
  queues are the likely difference, but that is not verified. Read the row as a check that the
  guest keeps its overlap.

## How the language-model rows are taken

On an M1 Max the GPU clocks up for a short burst and settles lower under sustained load. The 7B
model generates at 71-72 tok/s in its first run and 52-57 after, on both sides. The rows
therefore give the median of five back-to-back runs after two untimed ones: sustained speed,
which is what a long generation gets. The first runs of each model are in the logs.
